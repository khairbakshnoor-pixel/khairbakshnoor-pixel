### Engineering intelligent automation

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    P["Business problem"] --> A["AI agent<br/>voice · chat · RAG"]
    A --> W["Workflow<br/>n8n · APIs · webhooks"]
    W --> C["Connected systems<br/>CRM · calendar · data"]
    C --> H["Useful next step<br/>answer · booking · handoff"]
```

From voice-based lead qualification and appointment scheduling to CRM synchronization and knowledge-based support, I focus on the complete path from a real need to a reliable next action—not just a model or demo in isolation.

#### How I approach a build

| Stage | What I work through |
| --- | --- |
| **Understand** | Clarify the customer or operations problem, inputs, edge cases, and what a useful outcome means. |
| **Design** | Choose the right interaction, model, workflow, integrations, and human handoff points. |
| **Connect** | Link agents to approved knowledge, APIs, CRMs, forms, scheduling, and team notifications. |
| **Test** | Exercise realistic and failure-path scenarios; check data handling, guardrails, and next steps. |
| **Improve** | Refine prompts and workflows based on observed behavior instead of assuming a model is always right. |

**Areas I enjoy solving:** customer enquiry handling · lead qualification · onboarding and information collection · appointment routing · internal knowledge discovery · repetitive cross-tool handoffs.
